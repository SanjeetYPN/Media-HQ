Scaling playbook

  1. Pay for output, not time, offshore. Editors and clippers are per deliverable. Hourly pay is only for in-room work: filming, events, community.
  2. One interview, four assets. Spotlight carousel, quote graphic, reel cut, story sequence. Capture costs once; distribution compounds.
  3. Log or it didn't happen. Payouts run from this log on the 1st. That single rule makes the cost data real.
  4. Promote by owning a station. An intern who runs Select for a full month cleanly earns the Ship station. That is the career ladder, and it keeps good interns.
  5. Cut what doesn't convert. Monthly, rank formats by profile visits, DMs and applications per dollar. The bottom format loses its slot.
  6. Sponsors fund media. Every sponsor package names its content deliverables. Target: sponsor cash covers the monthly media payroll.
